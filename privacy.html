Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LumiMundo Kids ("we", "us" or "our") handles information when using our content publishing application and this website.

1. Information we process

When the authorized channel owner connects a social-media account, we may process basic account information supplied by the platform, such as display name, avatar, account identifier, authorization status and access tokens. We also process videos, captions and publishing metadata selected for upload.

This informational website does not offer user registration and does not intentionally use advertising cookies or behavioral tracking.

2. How we use information

We use the information only to authenticate the authorized owner, prepare and publish approved educational content, monitor publishing status, secure the service and diagnose errors. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

3. Children

Our videos are intended for children and families, but the publishing application is an internal creator tool for its authorized adult owner. It is not designed to collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child provided personal information to us, contact us so that we can investigate and delete it.

4. Sharing and platform services

Information is shared with connected platforms, such as TikTok or YouTube, only as necessary to provide requested authentication and publishing functions. Those platforms process information under their own privacy policies. We may also disclose information when legally required or necessary to protect the service and its users.

5. Storage, security and retention

Credentials and operational records are protected with access controls and are retained only for as long as needed to provide the publishing service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and maintain security. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your choices and rights

The authorized owner may disconnect a platform through that platform's settings. To request access, correction or deletion of information controlled by LumiMundo Kids, email us. We will respond in accordance with applicable law and may need to verify the requester's identity.

7. Changes

We may update this policy when our practices or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top will show the latest revision.
